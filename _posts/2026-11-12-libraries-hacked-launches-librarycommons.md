LibraryCommons brings together library locations, service information, news and data from across the UK, creating the foundations for a comprehensive, openly accessible library information platform.

<!-- more -->

{% include figure.html image="/assets/images/librarycommons-showcase.png" caption="LibraryCommons website shown across desktop and mobile devices, featuring the interactive library finder, map search, and branch service information." %}

Libraries Hacked is pleased to announce the launch of LibraryCommons, a new website designed to improve discovery, visibility and access to information about public libraries across the United Kingdom.

Building on the legacy of the British Library's LibraryOn service, recently closed down, LibraryCommons provides information on library authorities and branch libraries throughout the UK while integrating a growing range of news and library-related information sources into a single platform.

The aim of LibraryCommons is simple: to make it easier for library users, library staff, researchers, developers and sector organisations to find accurate, up-to-date information about public library services.

As the platform develops, LibraryCommons will introduce major new features, including:

- a UK-wide catalogue search through integration with [https://librarybooks.uk/](https://librarybooks.uk/)
- a searchable repository of library reports, strategies, research papers and policy documents from the past 30 years in accessible web formats
- aggregated events listings from library services across the UK
- automated monitoring of public library information using web automation
- data validation tools that identify inaccuracies and help prompt updates to information such as opening hours, contact details and service changes

LibraryCommons has been developed with openness, collaboration and sustainability at its core. The platform will support the library sector by providing a shared, searchable source of information while reducing duplication of effort and increasing the visibility of local library services.

Libraries Hacked is inviting library services, sector bodies and partner organisations to contribute information, suggest enhancements and collaborate on the future development of the platform.

LibraryCommons is available now at [https://www.librarycommons.org.uk/](https://www.librarycommons.org.uk/)
