Libraries Hacked websites and digital tools do not collect, store, or sell personal information. We do not require user accounts, logins, or personal contact details to access projects, data dashboards, or prototypes.

If you contact by email at [info@librarieshacked.org](mailto:info@librarieshacked.org), your email address will only be used to respond to your inquiry and will never be shared or added to marketing mailing lists.

## Analytics

We do not use advertising trackers, marketing cookies, or tracking pixels.

For general website usage monitoring, we use a self-hosted, privacy-first analytics platform ([Umami](https://analytics.librarydata.uk/)). This:

* Does not store persistent cookies or cross-site identifiers on your device.
* Does not collect personal data or track individual users across the web.
* Anonymizes IP addresses and collects only aggregate metrics (such as page views, referrer sources, and browser types).

## External services

This website contains links to external public library websites, open data repositories, GitHub, and partner platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical request data (such as IP addresses). This data handling is covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact at [info@librarieshacked.org](mailto:info@librarieshacked.org).
